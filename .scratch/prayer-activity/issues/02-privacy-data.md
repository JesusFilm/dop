# Privacy & data handling

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: —

## Question

Prayer requests are personal and sensitive. Decide the rules that protect people and that the form copy + storage design must honor:

- **Who can see a given request?** (Presumably only the one person assigned to pray for it — never public, never all-visible.)
- **Is the request shown with the submitter's name** to their assigned pray-er, or anonymized?
- **Consent / expectation**: does the submitter need to be told "one other person will see this and pray for you"? What does the form need to say up front?
- **Retention**: is the data deleted after the event? Same day? Who is responsible?
- **Org constraints**: any organizational policy (safeguarding, data handling, minors present?) that changes the above.

The outcome constrains ticket 06 (form + confirmation copy) and 05 (return flow), and the storage design in the stack decision.
