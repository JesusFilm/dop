# Scope & logistics

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: —

## Question

Establish the event facts every other decision leans on:

- **Headcount**: roughly how many people are expected to participate? (order of magnitude — dozens, ~100, more?)
- **Timeline on the day**: when do submissions open, when is the automatic reveal/assignment time, and when do people come back to view their assignment?
- **Do organizers participate** (submit + get assigned) or only run the event?
- **Physical model**: after assignment, does the pray-er just receive the request and pray privately/wherever they are, or do people physically find each other to pray together? (Affects whether names/contact need to be shown.)
- **QR display**: where/how is the QR code shown (poster, slide, handout)?

These are facts, not deep decisions — resolve fast so the assignment model and stack tickets can proceed.
